Monterosi — A legacy of craftsmanship

Monterosi began with a workshop on the Tuscan hills and a promise: build cars that marry artistry and performance. Across decades, Monterosi forged a reputation for lightweight chassis, distinctive coachwork and a relentless pursuit of driving purity.

Gianni Monterosi

Founder & Master Coachbuilder (1932–1998)

Our Story

Monterosi started in 1961 in a small artisan workshop just outside Florence. Gianni Monterosi — an intuitive designer and practical engineer — built his first two-seater coupe using lightweight aluminium panels and a custom-tuned V6. Word spread quickly among enthusiasts: Monterosi cars were beautiful, taut, and alive on the road.

1961 — Workshop Founded

Gianni Monterosi opens a boutique workshop and builds the first Monterosi 120, praised for its handling and handcrafted bodywork.

1972 — First International Recognition

Monterosi wins multiple endurance events in Italy and garners attention from collectors in Europe.

1988 — The Grand Touring Era

Introduction of the Monterosi Lusso — a comfortable, high-performance GT that becomes a symbol of Italian refinement.

2005 — Modern Craftsmanship

Monterosi reimagines its models blending modern materials like carbon fiber with traditional coachbuilding techniques.

2024 — New Dawn

Under a next-generation team, Monterosi launches a track-focused series alongside a limited-run electric Gran Turismo, honoring Gianni's designs with contemporary engineering.
